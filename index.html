Services

What Moaad LLC does

We help founders and small teams ship software with a clear scope: design the product, build it, and keep it running.

01

Software product design & build

End-to-end delivery of SaaS and web products—user flows, interface design, frontend and backend implementation—scoped tightly so the first version is usable.

02

Product interface design

Layout, typography, and interaction design for software products. Clear screens, readable hierarchy, and interfaces that stay calm under real use.

03

Maintenance & iteration

Ongoing ownership after launch: fixes, small features, performance work, and the operational upkeep that keeps a product trustworthy.

About

A registered software company

Moaad LLC is a single-member limited liability company organized in Wyoming. We operate as a software product studio: designing and building software for clients and for our own products.

Work is led by Moaad Ben Taher. Engagements stay intentionally small so delivery stays direct—from first sketch through production and follow-on maintenance.
